## 1. Tomorrowland the festival & brand

- Tomorrowland is a huge electronic music festival held in Boom, Belgium, now with spin‑offs in France (Tomorrowland Winter), Brazil and Thailand, built around a fantasy‑future mythos and world‑building. [tomorrowlandbelgium.press.tomorrowland](https://tomorrowlandbelgium.press.tomorrowland.com/tomorrowland-unveils-global-theme-consciencia)
- The organization has broadened into “Tomorrowland Leisure” and a wider “People of Tomorrow” brand, focused on joy, wonder, human connection, and immersive themed environments. [leisure.tomorrowland](https://leisure.tomorrowland.com)

**Illustrative events**

- Annual main festival in Belgium (two July weekends, ~400,000 attendees) with elaborate future‑themed stages and narrative “chapters” like the new global theme “Consciencia,” which connects editions across continents. [tomorrowlandbelgium.press.tomorrowland](https://tomorrowlandbelgium.press.tomorrowland.com/tomorrowland-unveils-global-theme-consciencia)
- Tomorrowland Winter in Alpe d’Huez, France (next edition March 21–28, 2026) combining ski resort life with the same fantasy‑future aesthetic and electronic acts. [tomorrowlandbelgium.press.tomorrowland](https://tomorrowlandbelgium.press.tomorrowland.com/tomorrowland-unveils-global-theme-consciencia)
- The Tomorrowland Foundation: funding music and arts schools for vulnerable kids in Nepal, India, Brazil, plus mobile school projects. [tomorrowlandfoundation.press.tomorrowland](https://tomorrowlandfoundation.press.tomorrowland.com/the-tomorrowland-foundation-blends-play-and-purpose-with-the-live-love-unite-puzzle)

## 2. The Love Tomorrow / Love Tomorrow Summit “movement”

This is the part that is closest to a “future movement” rather than just a party.

- “Love Tomorrow” is Tomorrowland’s own platform for positive impact on society and environment, working on sustainability, circular models, and behavior change with festivals, businesses, innovators, universities, and governments. [tomorrowlandaroundtheworld.press.tomorrowland](https://tomorrowlandaroundtheworld.press.tomorrowland.com/tomorrowland-welcomes-a-slate-of-internationally-renowned-thought-leaders-from-different-fields-that-will-be-hosting-15-inspiration-sessions-during-its-digital-festival-tomorrowland-around-the-world)
- They run the **Love Tomorrow Conference/Summit**, a day of keynotes and panels on “a better future” covering cleantech, health, AI, and sustainable culture, often timed around the festival. [lovetomorrow](https://lovetomorrow.com/love-tomorrow-summit/)

**Mission / code vibes (de‑facto bylaws)**

Not a classic membership club with bylaws, but their stated values look like a soft code:

- Core values: “Live Today, Love Tomorrow, Unite Forever” – focus on unity, creativity, and global community. [lovetomorrow](https://lovetomorrow.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/LT-Sustainability-Journey-2024.pdf)
- Vision: a world where “People of Tomorrow live as one — free to be themselves, connected by joy, and united in celebration of life,” while reducing environmental impact and promoting circular practices. [tomorrowlandaroundtheworld.press.tomorrowland](https://tomorrowlandaroundtheworld.press.tomorrowland.com/tomorrowland-welcomes-a-slate-of-internationally-renowned-thought-leaders-from-different-fields-that-will-be-hosting-15-inspiration-sessions-during-its-digital-festival-tomorrowland-around-the-world)
- Activities: sustainability projects (water, waste, recycling), supporting start‑ups and pilots, research into behavior and impact, and education via inspiration sessions. [lovetomorrow](https://lovetomorrow.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/LT-Sustainability-Journey-2024.pdf)

You can absolutely frame this as a **movement‑adjacent culture project**: not a cultish club, but a branded utopian community around future‑positive, sustainable living and tech.

## 3. Where AI shows up

AI is already woven into both the festival experience and the Love Tomorrow platform.

- BMW x Tomorrowland “Future Record” campaign: fans create personalized dance tracks via an AI system. A chatbot asks about mood, preferred genres and visions for the festival, then AI mixes a unique song and lyrics (via a ChatGPT interface) plus AI‑generated cover art and video for TikTok/Reels/Shorts. [press.bmwgroup](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/article/detail/T0425558EN/bmw-and-tomorrowland-create-a-whole-new-music-experience-with-the-help-of-artificial-intelligence?language=en)
- Love Tomorrow Conference 2024 launched “A Tomorrow We Choose,” inviting the community to co‑design a short film about a sustainable, connected future **using AI** to stitch together that collaborative vision. [lovetomorrow.press.tomorrowland](https://lovetomorrow.press.tomorrowland.com/love-tomorrow-conference-2024-launches-a-tomorrow-we-choose-campaign)
- Love Tomorrow Summit programming explicitly includes AI topics alongside cleantech, health, and sustainability, positioning AI as part of tools for a better future. [lovetomorrow](https://lovetomorrow.com/love-tomorrow-summit/)

So: they are not an “AI org,” but **they actively collaborate with AI tech (chatbots, generative media) and program AI as a future‑shaping theme**. [lovetomorrow.press.tomorrowland](https://lovetomorrow.press.tomorrowland.com/love-tomorrow-conference-2024-launches-a-tomorrow-we-choose-campaign)

## 4. Is Tomorrowland a club or organization with rules?

- Legally and structurally, Tomorrowland is a festival/entertainment brand with subsidiaries like Tomorrowland Leisure and the Tomorrowland Foundation, rather than a member club with published bylaws for individuals. [leisure.tomorrowland](https://leisure.tomorrowland.com)
- The closest thing to “rules and bylaws” are:
  - Values around diversity, unity, and inclusion (“People of Tomorrow,” “Live Today, Love Tomorrow, Unite Forever”). [tomorrowlandaroundtheworld.press.tomorrowland](https://tomorrowlandaroundtheworld.press.tomorrowland.com/tomorrowland-welcomes-a-slate-of-internationally-renowned-thought-leaders-from-different-fields-that-will-be-hosting-15-inspiration-sessions-during-its-digital-festival-tomorrowland-around-the-world)
  - Sustainability guidelines and projects (Recycle Club, water initiatives, Camp2Camp, circularity), aimed at changing both attendee behavior and industry practice. [lovetomorrow](https://lovetomorrow.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/LT-Sustainability-Journey-2024.pdf)

For your human‑AI‑future narrative, you can treat Tomorrowland / Love Tomorrow as:

- A **real‑world utopian stage set** where a corporatized, global “future of joy and unity” is marketed.  
- A place where AI is already part of the aesthetic and participatory layer (AI‑generated tracks, co‑created films), which you can contrast with darker AI realities in your doc ideas.
