# 📣 THE HEADLINE HOOKS MATRIX
### Project: The Human Story of A.I.
### Version: 1.0 (Refined for GitHub Vault)

This matrix is a "Formula Engine." Use these styles to turn any AI story into a high-impact headline based on your target platform.

---

## 🏗️ THE HOOK FORMULAS

### STYLE A: The "Documentary" Hook (Cinematic & Deep)
*Best for: Long-form episodes and Netflix-style thumbnails.*
*   **Formula:** [Object] + [Action] + [Existential Question]
*   **Example:** "The 1% Artist: When the robot carves, who owns the soul?"
*   **Example:** "The Parts Warehouse: Where the future of humanity is being built."

### STYLE B: The "Clickbank" Hook (Urgent & Fear-Based)
*Best for: Warnings, security explainers, and "Hard Truths."*
*   **Formula:** "This Is Why You Should Never" + [Human Action] + [AI Consequence]
*   **Example:** "This Is Why You Should Never Give an AI Both Your Camera and Your Calendar."
*   **Example:** "Why You Don't Let Your AI Assistant Auto-Fill Your Passwords on Sketchy Sites."

### STYLE C: The "Unprofessional YouTube" Hook (Curiosity-Gap)
*Best for: Shorts, viral clips, and reaction videos.*
*   **Formula:** [Number] + [Shocking Category] + "Caught on Camera" or "You Won't Believe"
*   **Example:** "Top 5 Wildest 'Karen vs. Self-Checkout' Freakouts Ever Caught on Camera."
*   **Example:** "We Tried Living Like We Were in ‘Her’ for a Week (Disaster)."

## 🛠️ HOW TO USE THIS MATRIX
1. **Identify the Story:** (e.g., A person uses an AI to write a resume).
2. **Choose the Platform:** (YouTube Short vs. Serious Documentary).
3. **Apply the Style:** 
   - *Documentary:* "The Synthetic Worker: The death of the human resume."
   - *Clickbank:* "Why You Should Never Let an AI Write Your Resume Alone."
   - *YouTube:* "I Let AI Apply for 100 Jobs—I Got Hired at 5... and Fired at 1."
